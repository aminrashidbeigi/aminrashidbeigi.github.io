## House rules

One question, eight people, sixty minutes. Cameras on. Nothing is recorded. What is said stays in the room; a short summary without names is published afterwards. Seats go first come, first served; once they are taken you can join the waitlist. If you cannot come, say so in advance and your seat goes to the waitlist.
