## House rules

One question, eight people, sixty minutes. Cameras on. Nothing is recorded. What is said stays in the room; a short summary without names is published afterwards. Seats are by application so each table has a mix of roles and experience. If you cannot come, say so in advance and your seat goes to the waitlist.
