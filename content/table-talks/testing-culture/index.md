Quality is easy to call everyone's job and hard to make anyone's habit. At this table we share what has worked, and what has not, in teams where testing is owned by the whole team instead of one person or one stage.

AI makes the question sharper. Assistants now write a growing share of our code, and often the tests for it too. That can make testing cheaper, or it can make it easier to stop thinking about quality at all. Some of what we will talk about:

- When AI writes the tests, who owns quality?
- Do generated tests catch real bugs, or only confirm what the code already does?
- How has code review changed now that more of the code is generated?
- Where has AI made testing easier in your team, and where has it added new risk?
