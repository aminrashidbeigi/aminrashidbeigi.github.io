Quality is easy to call everyone's job and hard to make anyone's habit. At this table we share what has worked in our teams, and what has not.

Some of what we will talk about:

- Whose job is quality in your team: QA, developers, or everyone?
- If you have QA engineers, what do they do that developers don't, and what should developers own themselves?
- Who writes the tests, and who decides a change is ready to ship?
- When a bug reaches production, who fixes it, and what changes afterwards?
- How do you keep a team caring about tests when deadlines are tight?
- And now that AI writes more of our code and tests, does any of this change?
